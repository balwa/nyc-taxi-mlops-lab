# S0: walking skeleton (Day 1, 10:30–12:30)

**Goal:** a push to `main` ends with the new version of the app answering on your app host's public IP. Nothing is smart yet: `/predict` quotes the static median (12.25 minutes) for every trip. What matters is the path from commit to running container.

## Recall check (5 minutes, no agent)

1. What does `terraform plan` read that `terraform validate` doesn't?
2. Why can't a GitHub-hosted runner reach a service on your EC2 host's private IP?
3. In Compose, what is the difference between `expose` and `ports`?
4. What does `permissions:` at the top of a workflow change about `GITHUB_TOKEN`?

## Given

- `contracts/`: the app API (`openapi.yaml`), names and ports (`runtime.yaml`), MCP tools. Frozen.
- `app/`: the backend with its contract tests. `deploy/`: the app host's Compose stack and Traefik config.
- `infra/modules/`: `host`, `rds`, `bucket`, `secret`. `infra/live/`: `versions.tf`, `variables.tf`, `terraform.tfvars.example`.
- `infra/bootstrap/state-bucket.sh`, `scripts/gh-secrets.sh`, `scripts/catch-up.sh`, `make check`.

## Build

1. **State:** run `infra/bootstrap/state-bucket.sh`, then `terraform -chdir=infra/live init -backend-config=backend.hcl`.
2. **Root module:** wire the modules in `infra/live/main.tf`, write the security groups in `security.tf`, and expose the outputs that `runtime.yaml` lists in `outputs.tf`. Plan, review, apply.
3. **CI** (`.github/workflows/`): `make check` on every push and PR; on `main`, build the `linux/arm64` app image and push it to GHCR.
4. **Runner:** your repo is public, so first make every fork-PR workflow wait for your approval (Settings → Actions → General → "Require approval for all external contributors"). Then register the app host as a self-hosted runner with label `lab-app` (`scripts/register-runner.sh`).
5. **CD:** after CI succeeds on `main`, deploy the new image on the app host with `deploy/compose.app.yaml`, then smoke-test `/version`.

## Decision points (one record each in `decisions/`)

- **Runner credentials:** the instance profile, or static session credentials in GitHub secrets?
- **Image tag scheme:** what identifies a deployable image, and what does a rollback point at?
- **Ingress scope:** who may reach port 80 on the app host?

## Done when

- `make check` passes locally and in CI, and `make agent-check` passes.
- `curl http://<app_public_ip>/version` shows the `git_sha` of your latest commit on `main`.
- `curl -X POST http://<app_public_ip>/predict -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"pickup_zone_id": 132, "dropoff_zone_id": 230, "pickup_datetime": "2024-03-08T17:05:00"}'` returns a quote, and an invalid zone returns 422.
- Fork-PR workflows require your approval for all external contributors.
- Your three decision records exist. Expect to defend the rejected alternative in each.

## Before 12:30 (session refresh)

Nothing in S0 needs your session credentials after `apply`: CI uses `GITHUB_TOKEN` and the runner uses the instance profile. After Start Lab, the hosts restart and the stack comes back by itself (with Elastic IPs, at the same address). Check that it did.
